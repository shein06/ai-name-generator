Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the Visual PDF Splitter safe for confidential documents? ＋
Yes, absolutely. OmniTools AI processes your PDF file 100% locally inside your web browser's virtual memory sandbox. Unlike other online converters, we do not upload your documents to any server, meaning your sensitive corporate and personal data remains completely safe on your device.
Does splitting a PDF reduce its original vector quality? ＋
No. This splitter copies and extracts pages natively as raw binary object streams using PDF-Lib. It does not rasterize your document layers into images, so all texts, fonts, charts, and vectors remain infinitely sharp and selectable.
Can I split password-protected PDFs? ＋
Currently, our offline tool can only process unencrypted, standard PDF files. If a document is locked with user password security, you will need to unlock it first before editing pages visually in this workspace.
