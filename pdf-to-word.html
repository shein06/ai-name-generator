Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this PDF to Word converter completely safe? ＋
Yes, absolutely. OmniTools AI processes your PDF file 100% locally inside your web browser's virtual memory sandbox. Unlike other online converters, we do not upload your documents to any server, meaning your sensitive corporate and personal data remains completely safe on your device.
Can I convert scanned PDFs or images into Word? ＋
This tool is optimized for text-based PDFs (documents where you can highlight text). Scanned PDFs or images do not contain structural text paths, so they cannot be extracted with this standard client-side reader without an OCR (Optical Character Recognition) engine.
Will the original formatting of the PDF be preserved? ＋
Our tool is designed primarily as a text extractor to keep files fast and lightweight. While it preserves paragraph layouts, lists, and general textual flows, it may discard complex table structures and graphic backgrounds to ensure the output remains clean and easily editable.
