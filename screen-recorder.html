Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does this online screen recorder have a watermark? ＋
No, this tool is 100% free with no watermarks, brand logos, or visual stamps overlaid on your recorded files. You get clean, professional-looking screen recordings.
Can I record audio from both my microphone and computer system? ＋
Yes! Under the 'Audio Source' dropdown, select 'Microphone + System'. When your browser requests screen sharing, make sure to check the 'Share System Audio' box in the sharing prompt window to enable full mixed recording.
Is there a time limit on the recordings? ＋
There are no artificial time limits set by our page. However, because recording occurs directly inside browser virtual memory, maximum video length may vary depending on your device's free RAM and local system resources.
