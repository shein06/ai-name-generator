Understanding Error Correction (ECL)

A unique feature of QR codes is their ability to sustain damage and still be scannable. If you are uploading a custom logo to put in the center of the code, you are technically "damaging" the center pixels. This is why we default our generator to Level H (High Error Correction), ensuring that even if $30\%$ of the code is covered by your logo, mobile phone cameras can still scan it flawlessly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the QR Generator free to use? ＋
Yes, our Advanced QR Generator is 100% free of charge. You can generate and customize unlimited high-resolution QR codes without any hidden costs or limitations.
What is the advantage of using High Error Correction (Level H)? ＋
Level H (High) allows the QR code to remain scannable even if up to $30\%$ of its surface is covered or missing. This is crucial when embedding custom logos in the center of the QR code so that smartphone cameras can still decode the link.
Can I generate transparent QR codes? ＋
Yes! Check the "Transparent BG" option under Background Color to make the white background transparent. This is ideal for designers overlaying QR codes onto brochures, menus, or existing graphic assets.
