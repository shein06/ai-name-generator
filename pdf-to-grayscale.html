Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is my uploaded PDF document safe? ＋
Yes, absolutely. OmniTools AI processes your PDF file 100% locally inside your web browser's virtual memory sandbox. Unlike other online converters, we do not upload your documents to any server, meaning your sensitive corporate and personal data remains completely safe on your device.
Does converting a PDF to grayscale reduce its file size? ＋
In most cases, yes. Converting colorful high-resolution image assets into grayscale JPEG formats decreases file sizes significantly, making the resulting document much easier to share via email or upload to portal systems.
Can I edit the text of the PDF after grayscale conversion? ＋
Because this tool flattens the PDF pages into image layers to ensure maximum print compatibility, the text is rasterized into image pixels. This means the resulting PDF text will not be searchable or editable, but it remains extremely crisp and clear for printing.
